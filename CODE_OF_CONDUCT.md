# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other channel — is expected to be respectful, constructive and considerate.

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Harassment, insults and discriminatory language are not accepted.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not follow this code, and may block participants who repeatedly ignore it. Report a concern privately to the maintainer through GitHub.
